## Usage in TalentPilot AI

These resumes are structured for ingestion into TalentPilot AI:
1. **Resume Parser Pipeline**: Automatic extraction of contact details, technical competencies, work history, and verifiable claims via `pypdf`.
2. **AI Interview Plan Generator**: Generates 8 targeted, competency-based questions tailored specifically to each candidate's resume claims.
3. **Multi-Agent Evaluation**:
   - Interviewer Agent: Adapts follow-up probes based on candidate answers.
   - Evaluator Agent: Scores depth, accuracy, and technical specificity (0 to 5 scale).
   - Consistency Agent: Validates candidate statements against resume claims.
   - Critic Agent: Eliminates bias and flags generic or unsubstantiated responses.
